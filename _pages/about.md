I am a second-year PhD student at [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://www.umass.edu/) (UMass Amherst), advised by Prof. [Chuang Gan](https://people.csail.mit.edu/ganchuang/). I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science with first-class honors from [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/index.html) (CUHK). Before starting my PhD, I worked on embodied AI, multimodal learning, and generative models with Prof. Chuang Gan and Prof. [Yilun Du](https://yilundu.github.io/), and on low-level vision and self/semi-supervised learning with Prof. [Chi-Wing Fu](https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/~cwfu/) at CUHK.

My current research focuses on **dexterous robotic manipulation**. I am particularly interested in how reinforcement learning, tactile sensing, and human guidance can enable robotic hands to perform challenging manipulation tasks such as grasping, in-hand reorientation, and tool use. My recent work explores **tactile-guided reinforcement learning** for dexterous manipulation and **shared autonomy for dexterous-hand teleoperation**, where learned policies assist human operators in executing difficult object-level motions. More broadly, my goal is to build robotic systems that can interact with the physical world with the adaptability, responsiveness, and dexterity required for real-world tasks.
